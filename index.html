About Colorful Stagle

Guess the name of the song from Hatsune Miku: Colourful Stage!

How To Play

You get 9 attempts to name the Colorful Stage song.

Click the play button at the top to start listening. The audio is a short excerpt from the song, usually the chorus. You start with only 1 second of audio. Each incorrect guess will let you listen to more of the song. After unlocking the full excerpt, incorrect guesses will give additional hints.

Hint Details

  • 1 Guess: +2 seconds (3s total)
  • 2 Guesses: +2 seconds (5s total)
  • 3 Guesses: +5 seconds (10s total)
  • 4 Guesses: +5 seconds (15s total)
  • 5 Guesses: Full Excerpt
  • 6 Guesses: Name of the Unit (Group)
  • 7 Guesses: Option to view the song's artwork (jacket)
  • 9 Guesses: Game Over

Searching

Use the bar at the bottom to search for a song. You do not have to be exact when searching and do not have to type special characters. You can type in the unit name (e.g. More More Jump) or it's abbreviation (e.g. mmj) to show songs by that unit.

Modes

You start in Daily mode. This will be the same song for everyone on that day. Daily song resets at midnight (local time). After completing daily mode, you can do Unlimited which chooses random songs.

Song Details

This game uses all songs from the English (Global) server. The song names may not match the names on the Japanese server.
